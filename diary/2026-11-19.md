## 2026-11-19: hh-rules moved to v0.26.0

The project moved from hh-rules v0.25.0 to v0.26.0 in an update round run by
hh-advise under the operator's `/hh-supervise`, the session opened by
`hh-client --update`. No rule or skill was gained; the `principles` rule
changed, and the shared `.shellcheckrc`, unchanged here since v0.25.0, was
replaced with the tag's. Every step of the v0.26.0 migration note said there was
nothing to do, so no migration item went into the plan. Every commit check the
